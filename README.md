# SOLSTICE HEAT

Take the wheel as Maya Reyes, an ex-stunt driver drawn into one more summer of jobs, debts, and dangerous alliances in Solstice Bay, a fictional coastal city. Explore the open streets on foot or behind the wheel among everyday traffic and pedestrians; cars, motorcycles, boats, jet skis, and a helicopter are all there to commandeer and drive.

Ten story missions unfold through conversations with the city's characters, from car chases and a casino vault to a waterborne pursuit and a final crew job. Crime raises a five-star wanted level, bringing foot patrols, cruisers, roadblocks, police helicopters, and armored vans into the hunt. Fight back with a pistol, shotgun, or SMG, and visit the arms dealer for supplies.

Build cash and respect through store robberies, taxi fares, courier runs, races, stunt jumps, and hidden packages. Spend your earnings at the garage or paint shop, climb the respect ranks, and check your mission list on Maya's phone. A minimap helps you find your way as the city moves through day and night and changing rain. Synthesized WebAudio effects and three radio stations soundtrack the drive, while localStorage saves keep your progress.

## Run it

With Node.js 22, start the included static server from the repository root:

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open [http://localhost:8083](http://localhost:8083) to play. Press Enter to start; use WASD to move, E to interact, and P to open the phone.
